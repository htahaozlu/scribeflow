# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
**ScribeFlow** community a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

We expect everyone to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and collaborative.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Behavior that is disrespectful, harassing, or otherwise harmful to others is not
tolerated.

## Standard adopted

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1**. The full text — including the complete enforcement guidelines and the
detailed standards of behavior — is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Scope

This Code applies within all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests,
and discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers by opening a
confidential report through GitHub, or by contacting the repository owner
(**@htahaozlu**). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter
of any incident and will follow the Contributor Covenant's
[Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
when determining consequences.
